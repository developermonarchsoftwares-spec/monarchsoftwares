SERVICE 04 / TRAINING & PLACEMENT

IT Training & Placement Support

Monarch Softwares helps aspiring IT professionals become job-ready and connects them with companies. Our career engineering programs immerse freshers and career-switchers in hands-on projects under expert mentorship. Trainees build a portfolio of project work and receive support with applications and interview preparation, while companies gain access to trained candidates matched to the skills they need.

TRAINING

Comprehensive Training Programs

Hands-On Curriculum

We teach industry-relevant skills — full-stack development, AI & ML, digital marketing, HR operations — through practical projects. Graduates don't just have certificates; they have code and campaigns to show.

Mentorship

FOR EMPLOYERS

Placement Support for Employers

Candidate Sourcing

We maintain a talent pool of trained freshers and experienced candidates. Share your hiring requirements and we'll work to shortlist qualified candidates. Ask us about current terms.

Requirement-Based Hiring & Training

Have specific needs? We can design and run a custom training batch. Share your job descriptions and we'll train a cohort aimed at the roles you need to fill.

Retention Focus

Our selection approach combines skills assessment and interviews with in-depth training, so candidates are better prepared to stay on and contribute long-term.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for your training programs?

We train fresh graduates and working professionals looking to upskill or switch careers. Basic computer literacy is helpful; we build on that foundation with technical and soft skills.

How does placement support work?

We help trained candidates prepare for and apply to opportunities, including interview practice. Employers can share their requirements and access trained candidates from our talent pool. Contact us for current terms.

How are requirements communicated to Monarch?

Companies can submit a brief of their open roles and required skills. We then source candidates and/or run a custom training batch targeting those skills — like outsourcing recruitment and training together.
