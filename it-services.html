FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your development process?

We work in agile iterations with regular demos and planning. From day one we map out milestones, run code reviews in Git, and keep you in the loop so you have visibility into progress.

Which technologies do you use?

Our stack includes modern web (React, Node.js, Python, and more), mobile (Swift, Kotlin, React Native), cloud platforms such as AWS, and database technologies. We choose the best tools for your project's needs.

How long does a project take?

Timeline depends on scope. Smaller projects, such as a brochure website, can launch sooner than larger platforms like a custom app or ERP. We provide an estimated schedule in our proposal.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes. Post-launch support, including bug fixes, performance tuning, and minor updates, is discussed and agreed as part of your project proposal, and ongoing maintenance options are available.
